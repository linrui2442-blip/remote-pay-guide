## 02 — What is a TRC20 address?
Source URL: `?src=short02`

**Hook:** A client asked for my USDT TRC20 address. What does that mean?

**Script:**
USDT is the asset. TRC20 tells you which network the sender plans to use. Your receiving wallet or platform needs to support USDT on that same network. Don't assume every USDT address works on every network. If you're getting paid in stablecoins for the first time, use the beginner guide in my profile.

**Visual:** message bubble “Send me your USDT TRC20 address” → highlight USDT = asset, TRC20 = network.

## 04 — USDT or USDC?
Source URL: `?src=short04`

**Hook:** Your client says they can pay USDT or USDC. Which one should you choose?

**Script:**
Don't choose based only on the name. Ask what networks the sender can use, then check what your receiving wallet or platform currently supports. USDT and USDC are different assets, and supported networks can differ. Confirm the asset, confirm the network, then copy the receiving address. Beginner checklist in my profile.

**Visual:** USDT card vs USDC card → network checklist.

## 05 — Biggest beginner mistake
Source URL: `?src=short05`

**Hook:** Someone is sending you USDT? Don't make this beginner mistake.

**Script:**
The biggest mistake is copying a deposit address before checking the network. A sender can transfer the same stablecoin over different blockchain networks. If the sending network and receiving network don't match, you can create a serious problem. Confirm the network first, then copy the address from the official receive or deposit screen.

**Visual:** Wrong: address first. Right: Asset → Network → Address.

## 07 — Overseas client pays in crypto
Source URL: `?src=short07`

**Hook:** My overseas client wants to pay me in crypto. What should I ask them first?

**Script:**
Ask three questions before you send any address: What asset are you sending? Which network are you using? What amount are you sending? Then choose a receiving method that supports that exact asset and network in your location. Don't let the word “crypto” make the process more complicated than it needs to be.

**Visual:** three questions appear one by one.

**Hook:** How can a freelancer receive a stablecoin payment safely?

**Script:**
Treat it like a payment setup problem, not a trading decision. Confirm the asset, confirm the network, choose a compatible receiving account or wallet, and for a large first payment consider a small test transfer. Once the test arrives correctly, the sender can send the remainder. Beginner guide in profile.

## 09 — I received USDC. Now what?
Source URL: `?src=short09`

**Hook:** I just received USDC. What can I do with it?

**Script:**
First, make sure the payment actually arrived on the expected network. Then your options depend on the service you use and your country: hold it, transfer it, convert it, or use supported local cash-out options. Don't rush into a trade just because the money is in crypto. Start with the payment basics first.
