Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

About Remote Pay Guide

Remote Pay Guide provides educational information about receiving USDT and USDC payments and operates a content publishing workflow for its own social media channels.

Website analytics

This website uses Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate website usage, such as page visits and interactions. Analytics data is used to improve the site and its content.

Google and YouTube account data

Remote Pay Guide uses Google OAuth to connect an authorized YouTube channel to a self-hosted publishing workflow. The requested Google/YouTube access is used only to authenticate the authorized account and to upload, publish, or manage video content that the account owner chooses to publish.

OAuth credentials and tokens used by the publishing workflow are processed by the self-hosted Postiz instance controlled by the operator of Remote Pay Guide. Google user data is not sold, rented, or used for advertising, credit decisions, or unrelated profiling.

Sharing and disclosure

Google user data is not shared with third parties except as necessary to operate the authorized publishing workflow or when required by law. Content intentionally published to YouTube becomes subject to YouTube's own privacy terms and visibility settings.

Data retention and revocation

OAuth tokens may be retained only as needed to keep the authorized YouTube connection active. The account owner can revoke access at any time from Google Account security settings. Revoking access prevents future use of the connection until it is authorized again.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Remote Pay Guide's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the site's analytics or publishing workflow changes. The latest version will remain available at this URL.
